### [1] [Hitting all maximal independent sets in $c$-hollow graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15486)
*Joshua Cooper, Isaiah Hollars*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究 $c$-hollow 图中击中所有极大独立集的最小顶点集 $\tau(G)$，构造了 $\tau(G)=\Omega(n^{1/3}/\log n)$ 的下界，并证明对 cographs 和 split 图类猜想成立。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Bollobás, Erdős 和 Tuza 在 1991 年猜想：若图 $G$ 是 $c$-hollow（每个极大独立集大小至少 $cn$），则 $\tau(G)=o(n)$。此前仅有平凡上界 $O(n)$ 和 Alon 关于最大独立集的下界，但缺乏对极大独立集族横贯的下界。该猜想与独立集结构、图论中的极值问题相关。

Method: 使用随机构造（random construction）方法：通过随机图 $G(n,p)$ 和精心选择的参数，构造 $c$-hollow 图并分析其横贯大小。对 cographs 和 split 图则利用其结构性质（如 cotree 分解、团-独立集划分）直接证明。

Result: 主要定理：对任意 $0<c<1$，存在 $c$-hollow 图 $G$ 使得 $\tau(G)=\Omega\left(\frac{n^{1/3}}{\log n}\right)$。此外，对 cographs 和 split 图，$\tau(G)\le 1$（即存在一个顶点击中所有极大独立集）。

Conclusion: 该下界是猜想 $\tau(G)=o(n)$ 的第一个非平凡约束，表明猜想若成立则下界不能太弱。同时，对 cographs 和 split 图猜想的强形式成立，揭示了图类结构对横贯大小的影响。

Future Work: 缩小上下界之间的差距：当前上界为 $o(n)$，下界为 $\Omega(n^{1/3}/\log n)$。进一步可研究其他图类（如弦图、完美图）中猜想的成立情况，或改进随机构造得到更优下界。

Abstract: Fix a constant $c$ with $0<c<1$. We say a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices is $c$-hollow if every maximal independent set of $G$ has size at least $cn$. Denote by $τ(G)$ the size of a smallest set of vertices $T\subseteq V(G)$ such that every maximal independent set in $G$ intersects $T$, i.e., $T$ is a transversal for the family of maximal independent sets. In 1991, Bollobás, Erdős, and Tuza conjectured that if $G$ is $c$-hollow, then $τ(G)=o(n)$. Using a random construction, we show there exist $c$-hollow graphs with $τ(G)=Ω\left(\frac{n^{1/3}}{\log n }\right)$, establishing the first nontrivial lower bound constraining the conjecture and complementing a closely related lower bound due to Alon for maximum independent sets. We also show the conjecture holds in a strong form for the class of cographs and split graphs.

### [2] [Edge complexity of graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15598)
*Vishal Gupta, Alex Iosevich, Joshua Iosevich, Benjamin Song, Haoyuan Tian*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文引入图的边复杂度（edge complexity）作为邻接矩阵在所有顶点标号下的最小傅里叶比，刻画了取等条件，并给出了乘积图上的估计、与熵的联系以及随机图上的渐近行为。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 图的边复杂度是衡量图结构在傅里叶分析下复杂性的新度量，与图能量、谱性质及编码理论相关。已有工作关注图能量和谱半径，但缺乏对傅里叶比最小化的系统研究。本文填补了这一空白，并建立了与熵和随机图的联系。

Method: 使用傅里叶分析、谱图论、群论（仿射对合、Singer差集）、图乘积（弱积、笛卡尔积、强积）的傅里叶比估计、概率方法（随机图浓度不等式）以及编码定理。

Result: 主要结果包括：
1. 刻画了固定标号下取等条件：邻接矩阵的傅里叶变换每行每列至多一个非零元，这蕴含正则性、极值邻接矩阵的每个正偶次幂是循环矩阵，以及连通分支的奇偶性限制。
2. 对每个素数幂 $q$，利用Singer差集构造了一个达到等式的 $(q+1)$-正则图，且不是阿贝尔Cayley图。
3. 建立了弱积、笛卡尔积和强积的傅里叶比估计，并证明互质阶弱积保持等式。
4. 利用傅里叶比恢复作为编码定理，得到低复杂度邻接矩阵的熵上界，并通过扰动完全图给出下界。
5. 若 $Np_N/\log N\to\infty$ 且 $\limsup_{N\to\infty}p_N<1$，则 $\operatorname{FR}_{\min}(G(N,p_N))$ 以概率趋于1为 $\Theta(N)$。

Conclusion: 本文系统建立了图的边复杂度理论，揭示了其与图的正则性、循环性及群结构的深刻联系，并为随机图提供了精确的渐近行为。该度量有望成为图论和傅里叶分析交叉领域的新工具。

Future Work: 论文未明确列出开放问题，但自然延伸包括：刻画所有达到等式的图类；研究边复杂度与图的其他谱不变量（如零度、能量）的关系；推广到有向图或加权图；探索在编码理论和量子信息中的应用。

Abstract: Gupta and Iosevich introduced the edge complexity of a graph as the minimum Fourier ratio of its adjacency matrix over all vertex labelings and bounded it below by graph energy divided by the square root of twice the number of edges. We characterize equality for a fixed labeling: the Fourier transform of the adjacency matrix must have at most one nonzero entry in each row and column. This implies regularity, circulancy of every positive even power of an extremizing adjacency matrix, and a parity restriction on connected components, and it gives equality results for certain Laplacian spectral projectors. We construct equality cases from affine involutions on cyclic groups. Singer difference sets yield, for every prime power $q$, an equality-attaining $(q+1)$-regular graph that is not an abelian Cayley graph. We also establish Fourier-ratio estimates for weak, Cartesian, and strong graph products, including preservation of equality under weak products of coprime orders. We use Fourier-ratio recovery as a coding theorem to obtain entropy upper bounds for low-complexity adjacency matrices and complement them with a lower bound obtained by perturbing complete graphs. Finally, a concentration argument shows that if $Np_N/\log N\to\infty$ and $\limsup_{N\to\infty}p_N<1$, then $\operatorname{FR}_{\min}(G(N,p_N))$ is of order $N$ with probability tending to one.

</details>

Its correctness can be checked directly from the tables in the paper or by either of two independent, short, standard-library verifiers supplied with the certificate.

### [4] [The minimum surface area of $k$ unequal boxes tiling a cube: sharp thresholds, a fault-free law, and a reduction to two dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15894)
*Diego Lago Gómez*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文确定了用 $k$ 个边长整数且维数多重集互异的轴对齐盒子无重叠地拼成 $n^3$ 立方体的最小总表面积 $T(n,k)$，完整解决了 $k=6$ 的情况，并建立了从三维到二维的约化定理及无故障平面定律。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 该问题源于离散几何中的最优拼装问题，与整数分拆、组合优化及材料科学中的表面积最小化相关。此前仅知道 $k$ 较小或 $n$ 特殊时的结果，缺乏一般规律。本文填补了 $k=6$ 的空白，并揭示了阈值与三角数的联系。

Method: 主要技术包括：无故障平面定律（fault-free law）通过精确计数跨越块、浮动块和立方体角点；约化定理（reduction theorem）利用无条件 slab lemma 将三维问题降为二维；二维问题中采用有限情形树（finite case trees）证明中间区域的加倍律（doubling law）。

Result: 完全确定了 $T(n,6)$：当 $5\le n\le 9$ 时 $T(n,6)=8n^2+2n+12$，当 $n\ge 10$ 时 $T(n,6)=8n^2+2n+6$，例外值 $T(3,6)=100$，$T(4,6)=148$。一般规律：对任意 $k\ge 4$ 和 $n\ge (k-2)(k-1)/2$，$T(n,k)=8n^2+2n+2(k-3)$，阈值恰为三角数。

Conclusion: 该结果完整刻画了 $k=6$ 时的最优拼装，并建立了从三维到二维的约化框架，表明此类问题在特定范围内可降维处理。无故障平面定律给出了内部接口面积的精确公式，为更一般情况提供了结构洞见。

Future Work: 论文未解决 $k\ge 7$ 时 $T(n,k)$ 的完整确定，尤其是阈值以下区域的行为。自然延伸是推广约化定理到更高维，或研究盒子维数多重集允许重复时的类似问题。

Abstract: Let $T(n,k)$ be the minimum total surface area of $k$ axis-aligned boxes with integer sides and pairwise distinct dimension multisets whose union is the cube $[0,n]^3$. We determine the column $k=6$ completely: $T(n,6)=8n^2+2n+12$ for $5\le n\le 9$, and $T(n,6)=8n^2+2n+6$ for all $n\ge 10$, together with the exceptional values $T(3,6)=100$ and $T(4,6)=148$. The threshold $n=10$ equals $1+2+3+4$, the least possible sum of four distinct stick lengths, and the general law holds: for every $k\ge 4$ and every $n\ge (k-2)(k-1)/2$, $T(n,k)=8n^2+2n+2(k-3)$, with thresholds at the triangular numbers. Three structural results support and extend these values. First, a fault-free law: the minimum internal interface of a partition of the cube into six boxes with no fault plane is exactly $2n^2+n$ for all $n\ge 3$ (OEIS A014105), proved by an exact accounting of spanning pieces, floating pieces and cube corners. Second, a reduction theorem: within an explicit range, the three-dimensional problem collapses to a two-dimensional one, $I(n,k)=n^2+W^*(n,k-1)$, where $W^*(n,m)$ is the minimum internal wall of a tiling of the $n\times n$ square by $m$ rectangles of pairwise distinct dimensions; the key ingredient is an unconditional slab lemma. Third, a doubling law in the middle regime of the 2D problem: $W^*(n,4)=n+4$ for $n=4,5$ and $W^*(n,5)=n+6$ for $4\le n\le 9$, proved by finite case trees; via the reduction theorem this gives computer-free proofs of the middle regimes of the columns $k=5$ and $k=6$. The lower bound for the main family does not use the distinctness of the pieces.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Erdős-Rogers 函数是极值图论中一个重要的函数，它衡量在 $K_{s+1}$-free 图中避免 $K_s$ 的最大子集大小。此前已知上界和下界存在差距，该工作填补了这一空白，确定了精确的阶。

### [7] [A weighted entropy approach for the quadratic inverse large sieve conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15311)
*Ernie Croot, Chi Hoi Yip*

Main category: math.NT

TL;DR: 改进了二次逆大筛猜想的下界，证明若 $A\subseteq[N]$ 满足 $|A|\gg\sqrt N$ 且 $|A_p|\le p/2+O(1)$，则 $A$ 包含 $\exp\left(c\frac{\sqrt{\log N}}{\log\log N}\right)$ 个二次像中的元素。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 二次逆大筛猜想是解析数论中的重要问题，它刻画了在平方根阈值处达到极值的集合的结构。Hanson 首次证明了无条件结果，但下界仅为 $\log N$。本文显著改进该下界，并研究密度依赖变体，与 Green--Harper 的稳健逆大筛猜想及逆 Goldbach 问题相关。

Method: 加权熵方法（weighted entropy argument）结合筛估计（sieve estimates），受 Croot--Mao--Pohoata--Sheffer--Yip 近期工作的启发。

Result: 定理：若 $A\subseteq[N]$ 满足 $|A|\gg\sqrt N$ 且对每个素数 $p$ 有 $|A_p|\le p/2+O(1)$，则 $A$ 包含 $\exp\left(c\frac{\sqrt{\log N}}{\log\log N}\right)$ 个形如 $\{an^2+bn+c:n\in\mathbb{Z}\}$ 的二次像中的元素。还证明了密度依赖变体及二集版本。

Conclusion: 该结果大幅改进了二次逆大筛猜想的下界，表明满足条件的集合必须与二次像有显著交集，深化了对大筛法极值结构的理解。

Future Work: 能否将下界改进为 $N^\varepsilon$ 或 $N^{1/2}$？此外，Green--Harper 的稳健逆大筛猜想仍待完全解决，本文的二集版本是重要一步。

Abstract: The quadratic inverse large sieve problem predicts that the examples sharp at the square-root threshold are essentially quadratic. Hanson proved the first unconditional result in this direction: if $A\subseteq[N]$, $|A|\gg\sqrt N$, and $|A_p|\le p/2+O(1)$ for every prime $p$, then $A$ contains $\gg\log N$ elements in the image of a single quadratic. We significantly improve this lower bound to \[ \exp\left(c\frac{\sqrt{\log N}}{\log\log N}\right). \] We also prove density-dependent variants, including a two-set version motivated by Green--Harper's robust inverse large sieve conjectures and their connection with the inverse Goldbach problem. Combined with a theorem of Elsholtz--Harper on hypothetical decompositions of the primes, our results show that any such decomposition would force both summands to have large intersections with quadratic images. Our proof combines a weighted entropy argument with sieve estimates, inspired by the recent work of Croot--Mao--Pohoata--Sheffer--Yip.

</details>
