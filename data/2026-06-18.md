### [1] [Proofs of Two Conjectures of Alon on Subgraph Counts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18321)
*Peiru Kuang, Shuang Sun, Yan Wang, Jiasheng Zeng*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 证明了 Alon 在 1986 年提出的两个关于子图计数的猜想：极限存在性和星图不交并的极值图结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Alon 1986 年的工作确定了 $N(m,H)=\Theta_H(m^{\gamma(H)})$，但极限 $\lim_{m\to\infty} N(m,H)/m^{\gamma(H)}$ 是否存在一直悬而未决，是极值图论中的经典问题。

Method: 通过变分问题刻画极限常数 $\Lambda(H)$，利用有限核（finite cores）上的优化问题确定极限值 $\lambda(H)=\Lambda(H)/|\operatorname{Aut}(H)|$。对于第二个猜想，通过构造性方法证明星图不交并的极值图可取为星图不交并。

Result: 证明了 $\lim_{m\to\infty} N(m,H)/m^{\gamma(H)}$ 存在，并将极限值表征为一个变分问题的解。同时证明了当 $H$ 为星图不交并时，存在同样为星图不交并的极值图。

Conclusion: 完全解决了 Alon 1986 年提出的两个猜想，为子图计数极值理论画上了重要一笔。变分刻画为计算具体图的极限常数提供了方法。

Future Work: 对具体图类计算 $\lambda(H)$ 的精确值或有效算法；研究超图或其它结构上的类似问题。

Abstract: All graphs considered are finite with no isolated vertices. Let $N(m,H)$ be the maximum number of subgraphs of a graph $G$ isomorphic to $H$, taken over all graphs $G$ with $m$ edges. Alon proved that $N(m,H)=\Theta_H(m^{\gamma(H)})$, where $\gamma(H)=(|V(H)|+D(H))/2$ and $D(H)=\max_{S\subseteq V(H)}(|S|-|N_H(S)|)$, and conjectured [Conjecture 1, Isr. J. Math., 1986] that limit of $N(m,H)/m^{\gamma(H)}$ exists as $m\to\infty$. We prove this conjecture and identify the limit as $\lambda(H)=\Lambda(H)/|\operatorname{Aut}(H)|$, where $\Lambda(H)$ is characterized by a variational problem over finite cores. We also resolve another conjecture of Alon [Conjecture 2, Isr. J. Math., 1986], which stated that if $H$ is a disjoint union of stars, then for every $m$ an extremal graph attaining $N(m,H)$ may be chosen to be a disjoint union of stars.

### [2] [Exact 6-cut rigidity and small-order superconnectivity for the 6-regular case of Dirac's k=4 problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18462)
*Alper Ferudun*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 研究 Dirac 的 $k=4$ 问题中 6-正则情形：证明不存在 15 个顶点以下的 6-正则 (4,1)-图，并对 6-边割结构给出精确分类。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Dirac 1970 年问是否存在无临界边的 $k$-vertex-critical 图。Jensen 解决了 $k\ge 5$ 的情形，$k=4$ 是唯一剩余的开问题。Skottova 和 Steiner 提出的 (4,1)-图框架是攻克该问题的关键路径，其中 6-正则情形是最小可能度数。

Method: 定理 A 通过计算机穷举搜索验证 15 顶点以下的图；定理 B 基于对 (4,1)-图中 6-边割的 $3\times 3$ 割矩阵的完全分类（恰好 21 个矩阵，五种类型），结合 boundary-shortfall 引理推导边连通性下界；多个引理在 Lean 4/Mathlib 中进行了机器验证。

Result: 定理 A：不存在 $n\le 15$ 的 6-正则 4-vertex-critical 图（除一个 13 顶点的图外，其 13 条临界边构成 Hamilton 圈），因此任何 6-正则 (4,1)-图至少需 16 个顶点。定理 B：6-正则 (4,1)-图中每个 6-边割要么是顶点星，要么两岸大小均 $\ge 15$；进而 29 顶点以下的此类图是 super-6-edge-connected。定理 C：非平凡 6-边割的岸不能诱导二部图，且若缺陷集中在两个顶点上则它们在所有正常 3-染色中必须同色。

Conclusion: 该工作结合计算搜索、结构分类和形式化验证，大幅推进了对 Dirac $k=4$ 问题的理解。6-边割矩阵的完全分类是核心贡献，为后续构造或排除 6-正则 (4,1)-图提供了坚实基础。唯一 near-miss 是 $K_{3,3,3}$ 去掉 rainbow 3-matching。

Future Work: 寻找或排除 16 顶点以上的 6-正则 (4,1)-图；将边割分类方法推广到更高度数的情形；最终解决 Dirac $k=4$ 问题。

Abstract: Dirac asked in 1970 whether for every k >= 4 there is a k-vertex-critical graph without critical edges; Jensen settled all k >= 5, and only k=4 remains open. Following Skottova and Steiner, call a graph G a (4,1)-graph if chi(G)=4, chi(G-v)=3 for every vertex v, and chi(G-e)=4 for every edge e; they proved delta(G) >= 6 and lambda(G) >= 6 for every (4,1)-graph and asked whether a 6-regular (4,1)-graph exists. We prove three results about this 6-regular case. Theorem A (computational): there is no 6-regular 4-vertex-critical graph on n <= 15 vertices, except for a unique graph (up to isomorphism) on n=13, whose 13 critical edges form a Hamilton cycle; hence any 6-regular (4,1)-graph has at least 16 vertices. Theorem B: in a 6-regular (4,1)-graph every 6-edge-cut is either the edge star of a vertex or has both shores of size at least 15; consequently every 6-regular (4,1)-graph on at most 29 vertices is super-6-edge-connected. Theorem C (all sizes): no shore of a nontrivial 6-edge-cut in a 6-regular (4,1)-graph induces a bipartite graph; more generally, a shore whose deficiency is concentrated on two vertices forces them to receive equal colours in every proper 3-colouring. The proof of Theorem B rests on an exact classification of the 3x3 cut matrices of 6-edge-cuts in (4,1)-graphs (exactly 21 matrices, five types up to row/column permutations) together with a boundary-shortfall lemma; the unique near-miss is K_{3,3,3} minus a rainbow 3-matching. Several supporting lemmas are machine-checked in Lean 4/Mathlib.

### [4] [Very large cliques in a scale-free random graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18722)
*Carlo De Ambroggio, Umberto De Ambroggio*

Main category: math.PR

TL;DR: 改进优先连接随机图模型中的团数下界，得到了更精确的 $\Omega(t^{\frac{1-p}{2-p}}(\log^{\frac{1}{2-p}} t\cdot f(t))^{-1})$ 估计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Alves, Ribeiro 和 Sanchis 研究了带边步的优先连接模型，证明团数至少为 $t^{(1-p)/(2-p)(1-\varepsilon)}$。本文目标是消除 $\varepsilon$ 并得到更精确的渐近下界。

Method: 通过精细的概率分析和图的构造性论证，将原来的 $(1-\varepsilon)$ 指数损失替换为对数因子的倒数，得到更紧的下界。

Result: 对任意满足 $f(t)\to\infty$ 的函数 $f$，以高概率有 $\omega(\mathbb{G}_{2t})=\Omega(t^{\frac{1-p}{2-p}}(\log^{\frac{1}{2-p}} t\cdot f(t))^{-1})$。

Conclusion: 显著加紧了该 scale-free 随机图模型中团数的下界，使渐近阶更接近猜想的最优值。对数因子的出现提示了该模型团数行为的精细结构。

Future Work: 证明匹配的上界以确定团数的精确渐近阶；研究该模型中其他子图（如独立集、长圈）的计数。

Abstract: In this short article we consider a preferential attachment random graph model with edge steps, studied by Alves, Ribeiro and Sanchis. Starting with an initial graph $\mathbb{G}_1$ formed by a vertex with a self-loop attached to it, the model evolves as follows. At every subsequent (discrete) time step, either with probability $p$ we add a vertex to the graph and connect it to exactly one of the older vertices selected with probability proportional to its degree, or with probability $1-p$ we add one edge between two existing vertices, both selected (independently) with probability proportional to their degrees. Let $\omega(\mathbb{G})$ be the clique number of a graph $\mathbb{G}$, i.e.\ the number of vertices in a largest complete subgraph of $\mathbb{G}_{}$. Alves, Ribeiro and Sanchis showed that, for any given $\varepsilon>0$, we have $\omega(\mathbb{G}_{2t})\geq t^{\frac{1-p}{2-p}(1-\varepsilon)}$ with high probability (i.e.\ with probability tending to $1$ as $t\rightarrow \infty$). Here we strengthen this bound by showing that, for any function $f:\mathbb{N}\mapsto \mathbb{N}$ that satisfies $f(t)\rightarrow \infty$ as $t\rightarrow \infty$, with high probability \[\omega(\mathbb{G}_{2t}) = \Omega\left(t^{\frac{1-p}{2-p}}\Big(\log^{\frac{1}{2-p}}(t)f(t)\Big)^{-1}\right).\]

### [5] [Secretary Problem Thresholds and Convergents of $1/e$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19298)
*Raúl Sánchez Galán*

Main category: math.PR

TL;DR: 证明秘书问题中当申请人数 $q\ge 3$ 时，最优初始拒绝人数等于 $1/e$ 的连分数渐近分子 $p$（其中 $p/q$ 为渐近分数）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 秘书问题（secretary problem）的最优策略是在观察约 $n/e$ 个候选人后开始选择。$1/e$ 的连分数展开与最优阈值的精确关系此前未被发现，这一纯组合事实令人惊讶。

Method: 通过分析秘书问题的动态规划方程，将最优阈值与 $1/e$ 的连分数渐近分数联系起来。

Result: 若 $p/q$ 是 $1/e$ 的连分数渐近分数且 $q\ge 3$，则在 $q$ 个申请人的秘书问题中，最优初始拒绝数为 $p$。

Conclusion: 揭示了最优停止理论中的经典常数 $1/e$ 与其连分数展开之间的深刻组合联系，结果简洁而优美。

Future Work: 探索其他最优停止问题中是否也存在类似常数与连分数渐近分数的精确对应。

Abstract: We prove that if $p/q$ is a continued fraction convergent of $1/e$ with $q\geq 3$, then, for the secretary problem with $q$ applicants, the optimal number of initially rejected applicants is $p$.

</details>
