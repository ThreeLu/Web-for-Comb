### [1] [Logarithmic convergence of finite projective planes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28890)
*Márton Borbényi, Panna Tímea Fekete, Aranka Hrušková, Ander Lamaison*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 肯定回答 Szegedy 的 Question 4：有限域射影平面的关联图序列 log-converges，且极限与某个随机图模型一致。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Szegedy 提出的图 log-convergence 是稠密图极限理论的一个精细概念，射影平面关联图是自然测试对象。

Method: 分析有限域上射影平面关联图的 log-convergence，并与特定随机图模型的极限比较。

Result: 证明射影平面关联图序列 log-converges，极限与某随机图模型相同，肯定回答 Szegedy 的 Question 4。

Conclusion: 高度结构化的射影平面关联图在 log-convergence 意义下表现得像随机图。

Future Work: 研究其他代数关联结构的 log-convergence；刻画 log-limit 的一般理论。

Abstract: In this paper, we study the so-called log-convergence of graphs defined by Balázs Szegedy (arXiv:1504.00858). We answer his Question 4 affirmatively: the sequence of incidence graphs of projective planes over finite fields log-converges, and the limit coincides with that of a particular random graph model.

### [2] [Impartial Combinatorial Games and the Nuclear Escalation Ladder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29015)
*Arnav Garg*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 用 impartial combinatorial game 建模 Herman Kahn 的核升级阶梯，证明单阶梯稳定性由一个同余控制，多战区联合稳定性在 normal play 下为各战区升级距离的 Nim-sum。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 核升级阶梯此前只有经典 payoff 型博弈分析，尚未用 impartial combinatorial game 理论处理。

Method: 按到核门槛的距离重标每一级，将阶梯化为 subtraction game，并以“不愿先动手”的教条选择 misère 约定；用向后归纳分析 misère quotient。

Result: 单阶梯稳定性由同余刻画（Thm 4.1）并给出阶梯设计推论（Cor 4.2）；normal play 下联合稳定性为 Nim-sum（Thm 5.2）；misère 下 Nim-sum 失效，两步升级的 misère quotient 为六阶 monoid $\langle a,b\mid a^2=1,b^3=b\rangle$、loss set $\{a,b^2\}$（Thm 6.3）。

Conclusion: impartial game 理论首次应用于核升级阶梯，给出稳定性的精确代数刻画。

Future Work: 处理多步升级的 misère quotient；将框架用于其他升级/威慑情景。

Abstract: We model Herman Kahn's escalation ladder as an impartial combinatorial game. Reindexing each rung by its distance to the nuclear threshold turns the ladder into a subtraction game, the most tractable class in combinatorial game theory, and the doctrinal fact that no side wishes to fire first selects the misere convention. We prove that single-ladder stability is governed by a congruence (Theorem 4.1) and derive a ladder-design corollary that makes the burden of first escalation a function of ladder length and escalation granularity (Corollary 4.2). For simultaneous theaters we show, under normal play, that joint stability is the Nim-sum of the theater-wise escalation distances (Theorem 5.2), a condition that is neither additive nor dominated by the most dangerous theater. We then show the Nim-sum reduction fails under misere play, introduce the misere quotient as its replacement, and prove by exhaustive backward induction that for two-step escalation the quotient is the order-six monoid with generators a, b satisfying a^2=1 and b^3=b, with loss set {a,b^2} (Theorem 6.3). To our knowledge, impartial combinatorial game theory has not previously been applied to nuclear escalation ladders; the existing game-theoretic literature on escalation is classical and payoff-based.
