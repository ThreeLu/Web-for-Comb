Title: Non-abelian hypercohomology of a group with coefficients in a crossed module, and Galois cohomology
Authors: Mikhail Borovoi
Categories: math.GR, math.KT, math.RT
Abstract: We develop a hypercohomology theory of a group with coefficients in a crossed module, and apply it to define abelianization maps for Galois cohomology of reductive algebraic groups.
