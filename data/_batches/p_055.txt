Title: Arithmetic exceptionality of generalized Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind
Authors: Derya Acar, Metin Azmaz, Vural Cam, Ömer Küçüksakallı
Categories: math.NT
Abstract: In this paper, we show that generalized Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind, associated with the root system $A_2$, are not arithmetically exceptional. We achieve this by studying the norms of certain cyclotomic elements that admit a parametrization of finite fields.
