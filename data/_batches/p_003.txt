Title: Counting Hamiltonian Paths in 3-Regular Planar Graphs
Authors: Ira Pohl, Larry Stockmeyer
Categories: math.CO, cs.DM, cs.DS
Abstract: We introduce two infinite families of 3-regular planar graphs. Both families are conceptual adversaries to the Pohl-Warnsdorf algorithm for finding Hamiltonians. We provide a closed form calculation of the number of Hamiltonians.
