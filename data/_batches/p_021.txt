Title: Two Families of $q$-Supercongruences from Watson's Transformation
Authors: Wei-Wei Qi
Categories: math.CO
Abstract: In this paper, we establish two families of $q$-supercongruences modulo the third and fourth powers of a cyclotomic polynomial by employing Watson's ${}_{8}\phi_7$ transformation, the creative microscoping method introduced by Guo and Zudilin, and the Chinese remainder theorem for coprime polynomials.
