Title: On the chromatic number of the union of comparability graphs
Authors: Maria Chudnovsky, Linda Cook, James Davies, Seokbeom Kim, Sang-il Oum
Categories: math.CO
Abstract: Resolving in a strong sense an old problem of Gyárfás from the 1980s on the union of two perfect graphs, we prove that for every pair of positive integers $d$ and $k$, there is a graph $G$ with clique number $k$ and chromatic number $k^d$ that is the union of $d$ comparability graphs.
