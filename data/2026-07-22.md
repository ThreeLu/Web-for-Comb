*Alexander Shramchenko, Vasilisa Shramchenko*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 提出一种从已知区组设计构造新区组设计的方法，并建立区组设计与对子系统之间的联系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 区组设计是组合设计理论的核心对象，与有限几何、编码理论等密切相关。现有构造方法多样，但缺乏统一框架。本文旨在通过一种新的构造方式，揭示不同区组设计之间的内在联系，并探索其与对子系统（如 Steiner 系统）的关系。

Method: 提出一种基于已知区组设计通过某种变换（如取补、添加点或块）构造新区组设计的方法，并利用对子系统的性质（如配对、覆盖）建立关联。

Result: 建立了若干区组设计之间的新联系，例如某些 $t$-设计可通过该方法从其他设计导出；同时证明了某些区组设计等价于特定对子系统，并给出了一个猜想。

Conclusion: 该工作为区组设计的分类和构造提供了新视角，并揭示了对子系统在组合设计中的潜在作用。

Future Work: 验证所提出的猜想，并探索该方法在构造更大类别的区组设计（如对称设计、可分解设计）中的应用。

Abstract: We propose a new way to construct one block design from another, thus linking some block designs in a new way. At the same time, we find relationships between block designs and systems of pairs. Some of these relationships are established, another one is conjectured.

### [2] [On the Cores of Uniform and Almost-Uniform $3$-Qualitative Independence Hypergraphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18674)
*Raina Mary Thomas, Yasmeen Akhtar*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 研究均匀和几乎均匀的3-定性独立超图与合并Johnson图的结构对应关系，并确定其核，特别解决了最小未解实例$3\text{-}QI(8,2)$的核性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 定性独立超图为覆盖阵列的存在性和结构分析提供了组合框架。理解这些超图的核对于图同态和着色理论至关重要，而均匀和几乎均匀情形下的核性质尚未完全刻画。

Method: 利用合并Johnson图的结构对应，结合强独立集分类、强独立数、强色数和最大3-团大小的计算。

Result: 对于$3\text{-}QI(8,2)$，分类了所有强独立集，确定了强独立数，并证明它是一个核。对于$n>8$，给出了$3\text{-}UQI(n,2)$和$3\text{-}AUQI(n,2)$成为核的充分条件。

Conclusion: 该结果推进了对定性独立超图核结构的理解，为覆盖阵列的构造提供了理论支撑。

Future Work: 进一步刻画所有$n$下$3\text{-}UQI(n,2)$和$3\text{-}AUQI(n,2)$的核，并推广到更高阶的定性独立超图。

Abstract: Qualitative independence hypergraphs provide a useful combinatorial framework for analyzing the existence and structure of covering arrays. In this work, we study the \emph{uniform} and \emph{almost-uniform $3$-qualitative independence hypergraphs} $3\text{-}UQI(n,2)$ and $3\text{-}AUQI(n,2)$, and establish a structural correspondence between these families and merged Johnson graphs, with emphasis on their cores. Focusing on the smallest unresolved instance, $3\text{-}QI(8,2)$, we classify all of its strongly independent sets and determine its strong independence number. Using this, along with its strong chromatic number and the size of the largest $3$-clique, we show that $3\text{-}QI(8,2)$ is a core. For $n>8$, we further identify sufficient conditions under which $3\text{-}UQI(n,2)$ and $3\text{-}AUQI(n,2)$ are cores.

### [3] [On the Computational Complexity of Local and Global Covering Numbers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18833)
*Miriam Goetze, Lucas Schwebler*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究了图H被二部图或完全二部图覆盖的全局与局部覆盖数的计算复杂性，给出了局部覆盖数的下界并证明了其NP-hard性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 覆盖数问题在图论和组合优化中具有基础重要性，但局部与全局覆盖数的计算复杂性差异尚未被充分理解。Harary, Hsu和Miller给出了全局二部覆盖数的下界，但局部版本缺乏类似结果。Fishburn和Hammer提出了确定局部完全二部覆盖数复杂性的问题。此外，Knauer和Ueckerdt询问是否存在有限单调图类使得全局覆盖数易计算而局部覆盖数难计算。本文填补了这些空白。

Method: 使用分数色数的对偶性给出局部二部覆盖数的下界；通过将3-SAT归约到局部完全二部覆盖数问题证明NP-hardness；构造一个有限单调图类展示全局与局部覆盖数的复杂性分离。

Result: 1. 对于任意图$H$，$c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{B}}(H) \ge \lceil \chi_f(H) \rceil$，其中$\chi_f(H)$是分数色数，且该界是紧的。2. 确定$c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{B}_\mathrm{c}}(H)$是NP-hard的。3. 存在一个有限单调图类$\mathcal{G}$使得$c_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$可在常数时间内计算，而$c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$是NP-hard的。

Conclusion: 本文揭示了局部覆盖数在计算上通常比全局覆盖数更难，即使对于简单的图类（如完全二部图）也是如此。这为理解覆盖问题的复杂性景观提供了新视角，并回答了Fishburn-Hammer和Knauer-Ueckerdt提出的问题。

Future Work: 未来工作可探索其他图类（如弦图、完美图）的局部覆盖数复杂性，或研究局部覆盖数的近似算法。此外，对于有限单调图类，能否刻画使得局部覆盖数易计算的条件？

Abstract: The global and local $\mathcal{G}$-covering number $c_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$ and $c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$ encode how well the edges of a graph $H$ can be covered with graphs from a graph class $\mathcal{G}$: in the global setting, we minimize the number of graphs from $\mathcal{G}$ required, in the local setting how often a vertex is hit by the graphs of the cover. Within this work we consider for $\mathcal{G}$ the graph classes $\mathcal{B}$ of all bipartite and $\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{c}}$ of all complete bipartite graphs. We give a tight lower bound on $c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{B}}(H)$ in terms of the fractional chromatic number of $H$, thereby giving a local analogue of a result by Harary, Hsu and Miller. Answering a question by Fishburn and Hammer, we show that it is NP-hard to determine $c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{c}}}(H)$. Further, we provide a finite and monotone graph class $\mathcal{G}$ such that $c_{\mathrm{g}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$ can be computed in constant time for every graph $H$ while determining $c_{\mathrm{l}}^{\mathcal{G}}(H)$ is NP-hard. This yields a natural example to a question raised by Knauer and Ueckerdt.

</details>


### [4] [Stretched exponential asymptotics for bases of triangular bootstrap percolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18901)
*Andrew Elvey Price, Juliette Schabanel, Paul Thévenin*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究了三角形网格上的bootstrap percolation过程，证明了大小为$n$的基数的生成函数满足代数微分方程，并推导出基数$t_n$的渐近行为为拉伸指数形式$t_n \sim c n! e^{\sqrt{12n}} n^{5/12}$。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Bootstrap percolation是统计物理和组合学中的重要模型，研究最小渗透集（即基）的计数问题具有理论意义。此前已知基与避免模式$(12,12)$和$(231,312)$的$3$-排列一一对应，但这类排列的渐近计数尚未解决。本文填补了这一空白，首次给出了拉伸指数渐近行为的严格证明。

Method: 主要技术包括：利用组合结构建立基的生成函数的代数微分方程；通过分析该微分方程的奇异行为，应用鞍点法和奇异分析得到渐近展开。

Result: 设$t_n$为三角形网格$\mathfrak{T}_n$中大小为$n$的基的个数，则存在常数$c>0$使得$t_n \sim c n! e^{\sqrt{12n}} n^{5/12}$。

Conclusion: 该结果首次证明了避免特定模式的$3$-排列的计数具有拉伸指数渐近行为，为模式避免排列的渐近分析提供了新范例，并展示了bootstrap percolation与排列模式避免之间的深刻联系。

Future Work: 论文未明确提及未来工作，但自然延伸包括：确定常数$c$的精确值；推广到其他网格或更高维度的bootstrap percolation；研究其他模式避免排列的渐近行为。

Abstract: In this paper, we study a bootstrap percolation process on the finite triangular grid $\mathfrak{T}_n$ of side length $n$. We say that a subset $η$ of points in $\mathfrak{T}_n$ percolates if the final configuration, starting from $η$, is the whole grid $\mathfrak{T}_n$. A basis of size $n$ is then a subset of points of $\mathfrak{T}_n$ of minimum cardinality which percolates. In this paper, we first prove that the generating function counting bases satisfies an algebraic differential equation. Then, by analysing a modified version of this equation, we prove that the number $t_n$ of bases of size $n$ exhibits a stretched exponential asymptotic behaviour. More precisely, we show that $t_n \sim c n!e^{\sqrt{12n}}n^{5/12}$, for some constant $c>0$. These bases were recently shown by the second author to be in bijection with $3$-permutations avoiding the patterns $(12, 12)$ and $(231, 312)$, so this represents to our knowledge the first proven example of an asymptotic stretched exponential appearing in the study of pattern avoiding permutations.

</details>
