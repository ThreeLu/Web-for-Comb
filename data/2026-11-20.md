*Juho Lee*

Main category: math.CO

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 图分解为星图是经典组合设计问题，与 Tarsi 的猜想及图的度条件分解密切相关。此前 Zhao 和 Wu 给出了完全图 $K_{2k-1}$ 的反例，但完全多部图类的最优阈值尚不清楚，本文填补了这一空白。

Method: 利用 Tarsi 的充分条件证明完全图的分解；对完全多部图，结合有限计算验证与 extension argument 处理 $k=3$ 的情形，并构造完全三部图 $G_k=K_{k^2-k-1,k-2,1}$ 作为反例。

Result: 证明 $K_n$ 在 $n\ge2k$ 时可分解为 $\{K_{1,k},K_{1,k+1}\}$，结合 $K_{2k-1}$ 的反例得到完全图的最优阈值 $4k-3$。对完全多部图，$k=3$ 时 $\sigma(G)\ge9$ 充分，且 $G_k$ 满足 $\sigma(G_k)=k^2-1$ 但无此分解，故 $c_k\ge k^2$；因此 $k=3$ 时该类最优阈值为 $9$。

Conclusion: 结果确定了完全图的最优阈值，并揭示了完全多部图类中 $k=3$ 与 $k\ge4$ 的显著差异，说明度条件在更广图类中的阈值可能更高。

Future Work: 论文留下两个 open problems：证明 $k\ge4$ 时完全多部图中 $\sigma(G)\ge k^2$ 的充分性，以及确定一般图的精确 $c_k$ 值。

Abstract: We study minimum degree-sum conditions for decompositions into stars of sizes $k$ and $k+1$, where $k\ge3$. Write $σ(G)=\min_{uv\in E(G)}(d(u)+d(v))$, and let $c_k$ be the least integer threshold guaranteeing a $\{K_{1,k},K_{1,k+1}\}$-decomposition for every finite simple graph. Using Tarsi's sufficient condition, we prove that $K_n$ admits such a decomposition whenever $n\ge2k$. Together with the obstruction $K_{2k-1}$ of Zhao and Wu, this gives the optimal integer threshold $4k-3$ for complete graphs. For complete multipartite graphs, we establish the sufficiency of $σ(G)\ge9$ when $k=3$ by a finite computational verification and an extension argument. For every $k\ge3$, the complete tripartite graph $G_k=K_{k^2-k-1,k-2,1}$ satisfies $σ(G_k)=k^2-1$ and has no such decomposition. Consequently, $c_k\ge k^2$, even when the class is restricted to complete multipartite graphs. The optimal integer threshold in this class is therefore $9$ for $k=3$. Sufficiency of $σ(G)\ge k^2$ for complete multipartite graphs with $k\ge4$, and the exact values of $c_k$ for general graphs, remain open in this study.
