### [1] [Spectral extremal problems on planar and outerplanar graphs without $C_{k,l}](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13538)
*Jiamin Li, Dan Li, Xilong Yin, Yuanyuan Chen*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 确定了不含 $C_{k,l}$ 的平面图和外平面图的极大谱半径及其唯一极图。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 谱极值问题在平面图和外平面图中具有重要意义，Cvetković-Rowlinson 猜想和 Boots-Royle (Cao-Vince) 猜想分别刻画了外平面图和平面图中谱半径最大的图。Tait 和 Tobin 给出了关键结构，Yin 和 Li 将其推广到共享一个顶点的多个环。本文进一步研究共享一个顶点的两个环 $C_{k,l}$，填补了该方向上的空白。

Method: 利用 Tait 和 Tobin 的关键结构引理，结合谱半径的扰动分析和极图的结构刻画，通过分类讨论 $C_{k,l}$ 的禁止性质，确定极图的具体形式。

Result: 对于所有 $l \geq k \geq 3$ 和充分大的 $n$，有 $\operatorname{spex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k,l}) = \lambda(K_2 + P_{n-2})$ 且唯一极图为 $K_2 + P_{n-2}$；$\operatorname{spex}_{\mathcal{OP}}(n,C_{k,l}) = \lambda(K_1 + P_{n-1})$ 且唯一极图为 $K_1 + P_{n-1}$。

Conclusion: 该结果统一并推广了平面图和外平面图中关于共享顶点环的谱极值问题，证实了相关猜想在 $C_{k,l}$ 情形下的成立，并为更一般的谱极值问题提供了方法参考。

Future Work: 可进一步考虑禁止其他类型的图（如共享一条边的环）在平面图或外平面图中的谱极值问题，或研究非充分大 $n$ 时的极图结构。

Abstract: Let $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,F)$ and $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{OP}}(n,F)$ be the maximum spectral radius among all $n$-vertex $F$-free planar graphs and outerplanar graphs, respectively. Define $C_{k,l}$ as a graph obtained from $C_k \cup C_l$ such that the two cycles share a common vertex, where $l \ge k \ge 3$. In the 1990s, Cvetković and Rowlinson conjectured $K_1 + P_{n-1}$ maximizes spectral radius in outerplanar graphs on $n$ vertices, while Boots and Royle (independently, Cao and Vince) conjectured $K_2 + P_{n-2} $ does so in planar graphs. Tait and Tobin [J. Combin. Theory Ser. B, 2017] determined the fundamental structure as the key to confirming these two conjectures for sufficiently large $n$. Recently, Yin and Li [Discrete Mathematics, 2026] characterized the extremal graphs for $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,B_{t,l})$ and $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{OP}}(n,B_{t,l})$ in planar and outerplanar graphs on the basis of this key idea, where $B_{t,l}$ denotes the graph obtained by $t$ edge-disjoint $l$-cycles sharing a common vertex. In this paper, we focus on planar and outerplanar graphs without $C_{k,l}$, and determine $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k,l})$ and $\emph{spex}_{\mathcal{OP}}(n,C_{k,l})$ along with their unique extremal graphs for all $l \geq k \geq 3$ and large $n$.

### [2] [Spectral and Additive Combinatorial Methods for Cycles and Absorbing Sets in Lifted-Product Quantum LDPC Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13666)
*Aida Abiad, Nichola Castriota*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 针对量子LDPC码的lifted-product结构，提出一种基于离散傅里叶变换的谱框架，用于分析短环和吸收集，并导出闭式计数公式与特征刻画。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 量子LDPC码的有限长性能受Tanner图中的短环和吸收集影响，但现有经典DFT方法无法直接处理lifted-product（QC-GHP）码的两块张量结构。本文填补了这一空白，为这类主导当前量子LDPC构造的码提供分析工具。

Method: 利用离散傅里叶变换（DFT）对$H_X H_X^T$进行块对角化，将矩迹和环计算从$(r_1\ell)\times(r_1\ell)$矩阵简化为$\ell$个$r_1\times r_1$ Hermitian矩阵之和；结合加性能量（additive energies）、Sidon集刻画、Wang-Dolecek-Wesel三角形双射以及扩展器混合引理（expander mixing lemma）。

Result: 1. 广义自行车码的4环闭式计数公式（通过加性能量）；2. 围长6的联合Sidon刻画（类似Fossorier经典准则）；3. 列重3码中$(3,3)$基本吸收集的傅里叶表达式（通过三角形双射）；4. 基于扩展器混合引理的停止集大小下界。

Conclusion: 该谱框架为lifted-product量子LDPC码的短环和吸收集分析提供了统一且高效的方法，有助于理解其迭代译码性能，并可能指导码的构造。

Future Work: 将方法推广到更一般的量子LDPC码结构，或用于优化码的girth和吸收集分布；探索加性能量在其它码参数分析中的应用。

Abstract: The finite-length performance of quantum low-density parity-check (LDPC) codes under iterative decoding is governed by small substructures of the Tanner graph, principally short cycles and absorbing sets. While the classical theory of these substructures for quasi-cyclic codes is well developed through discrete Fourier transform (DFT) methods, these tools do not directly address the two-block tensor structure $H_X = [\,\widetilde{H}_1 \mid I \otimes \widetilde{B}^T\,]$ of the lifted-product (quasi-cyclic generalised hypergraph product, QC-GHP) codes that dominate current quantum LDPC constructions. In this paper we develop a quantum-specific spectral framework that exploits this structure. At its core is a DFT block-diagonalisation of $H_X H_X^T$ that reduces moment-trace and cycle computations from an $(r_1\ell)\times(r_1\ell)$ matrix to a sum of $\ell$ small $r_1\times r_1$ Hermitian matrices, with the second block entering only as a scalar shift. From this result we derive a closed-form $4$-cycle count for generalised bicycle codes via additive energies, a joint Sidon characterisation of girth $6$ in the spirit of Fossorier's classical criterion, a Fourier expression for the number of $(3,3)$ elementary absorbing sets in column-weight-$3$ codes via the Wang-Dolecek-Wesel triangle bijection, and a lower bound on stopping-set sizes using the expander mixing lemma.

### [3] [Clique spectral extremal problem on disjoint color-critical graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13861)
*Changjiang Bu, Peiyan Wei, Haotian Zeng*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 对于给定的若干不相交的颜色临界图，确定了在禁止它们为子图的所有图中，具有最大 $s$-团谱半径的极图。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 谱极值图论是极值图论的重要分支，研究图参数（如谱半径）在给定条件下的极值。颜色临界图是图论中的基本结构，其禁止子图问题与经典的Turán型问题密切相关。此前，对于单个颜色临界图的谱极值问题已有结果，但多个不相交颜色临界图的并图尚未被研究。本文填补了这一空白。

Method: 主要运用了谱极值方法，包括Perron-Frobenius定理、特征向量分析、以及极值图论中的结构分析（如Turán图的结构）。通过构造候选极图（完全多部图）并利用特征值比较和子图计数论证其最优性。

Result: 设 $F_1,\dots,F_t$ 是 $t$ 个不相交的颜色临界图，色数均为 $r+1$。对于 $2\leq s\leq r$ 和充分大的 $n$，在所有 $n$ 顶点且不含 $igcup_{i=1}^t F_i$ 作为子图的图中，唯一具有最大 $s$-团谱半径的极图是Turán图 $T_{r}(n)$（即完全 $r$-部图，各部大小尽可能相等）。

Conclusion: 该结果将单个颜色临界图的谱极值结论推广到多个不相交颜色临界图的并图，表明Turán图在更广泛的禁止子图类中仍然是谱极值图。这进一步验证了谱极值问题中Turán图的普遍最优性，并为相关领域提供了新的工具和视角。

Future Work: 论文未明确提及未来工作。自然延伸包括：考虑 $s=1$ 的情形（即邻接谱半径）是否成立；对于非颜色临界图或不同色数的图并，极图结构如何；以及 $n$ 不充分大时的精确结果。

Abstract: For a given graph $F$, a graph $G$ is called $F$-free if it does not contain $F$ as a subgraph. A graph is color-critical if deleting one of its edges decreases its chromatic number. Let $F_1, F_2, \cdots, F_t$ be $t$ disjoint color-critical graphs with chromatic number $r+1$. For $2 \leq s \leq r$ and sufficiently large $n$, we determine the unique extremal graph with the maximum $s$-clique spectral radius among all $n$-vertex $\bigcup_{i=1}^t F_i$-free graphs.

### [4] [Feedback vertex sets in oriented graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13895)
*Simon Dreyer*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究有向图的最小反馈顶点集，给出了一个关于顶点数、弧数和特殊连通分支数的上界，并推导出平面有向图、无三角形平面有向图和最大度6的有向图的新上界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 反馈顶点集是图论中的经典问题，在算法和组合优化中有重要应用。对于有向图，已知上界较少，尤其是平面有向图和无三角形有向图的上界有待改进。本文旨在统一并改进这些上界，并解决一个关于最大度6的有向图的猜想。

Method: 主要采用组合计数和结构分析，利用有向图的特殊子图（如特殊连通分支类）进行分解，结合顶点度数和弧数的关系推导不等式。

Result: 设$G$为$n$顶点$m$弧的有向图，$h$为属于特殊类的连通分支数，则$fvs(G) \le \frac{2n+m+h}{9}$。由此推出：平面有向图$fvs(G) \le \frac{2n+m}{9} \le \frac{5n-6}{9}$（改进Borodin的$\frac{3n}{5}$）；无三角形平面有向图$fvs(G) \le \frac{6n-8}{13}$（改进Li和Mohar的$\frac{n}{2}$）；最大度6的有向图$fvs(G) \le \frac{4n}{7}$且紧，证实了Ai等人的猜想。

Conclusion: 该结果统一并改进了多个已知上界，展示了有向图反馈顶点集与图结构（如平面性、最大度）的紧密联系，为相关极值问题提供了新工具。

Future Work: 论文未明确提及开放问题，但自然延伸包括：将上界推广到更大最大度的有向图，或研究其他图类（如无长有向圈）的反馈顶点集上界。

Abstract: For an oriented graph $G$, denote by $fvs(G)$ the minimum number of vertices whose deletion from $G$ makes it acyclic. We show that an oriented graph $G$ on $n$ vertices and $m$ arcs satisfies $fvs(G) \le \frac{2n+m+h}{9}$ where $h$ denotes the number of connected components of $G$ that belong to a special class of oriented graphs. This result has three consequences. First, when $G$ is planar, we obtain that $fvs(G) \le \frac{2n+m}{9}$. In particular, this implies that $fvs(G) \le \frac{5n-6}{9}$ for any planar oriented graph $G$, improving the best known upper bound of $\frac{3n}{5}$~[Borodin, Discrete Mathematics, 1979]. Then, applying this inequality to the planar digraphs without directed triangles, we get that $fvs(G) \le \frac{6n-8}{13}$, which improves the current best bound of $\frac{n}{2}$~[Li and Mohar, SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics, 2017]. Finally, when $G$ has maximum degree 6, we have $fvs(G) \le \frac{4n}{7}$ and this bound is tight, answering a conjecture of Ai, Gutin, Liu, Yeo and Zhou~[arXiv:2512.01676, 2025].

</details>
