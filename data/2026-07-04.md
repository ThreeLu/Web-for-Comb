### [1] [The exact generalized Turán number for \(C_6\) in \(C_8\)-free graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03856)
*Zian Chen, Jinghua Deng*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 确定 $C_8$-free 图中 $C_6$ 的精确 generalized Turán 数 $\mathrm{ex}(n,C_6,C_8)=6\binom{n-3}3+12(n-5)$，唯一极值图为 $K_3\vee(K_2\cup I_{n-5})$。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Gerbner–Győri–Methuku–Vizer 证明 $\mathrm{ex}(n,C_6,C_8)=\Theta(n^3)$ 并猜测无限制问题与二部情形同阶，精确值待定。

Method: $C_8$-free 图的 codegree 分解：linear-codegree 图中三角形的 packing lemma 恢复几乎生成的公共邻域，defect-absorption 论证把 stability 升级到精确极值图。

Result: 对充分大 $n$，$\mathrm{ex}(n,C_6,C_8)=6\binom{n-3}3+12(n-5)$，唯一极值图 $K_3\vee(K_2\cup I_{n-5})$。

Conclusion: $C_8$-free 图中 $C_6$ 计数的精确极值被确定。

Future Work: 处理其他 $C_{2k}$-free 中 $C_{2\ell}$ 的精确 generalized Turán 数。

Abstract: For graphs $F$ and $H$, let $\ex(n,F,H)$ denote the maximum number of copies of $F$ in an $n$-vertex $H$-free graph. Gerbner, Győri, Methuku and Vizer proved that $\ex(n,C_6,C_8)=Θ(n^3)$ and predicted that the unrestricted problem should have the same first-order asymptotics as the bipartite one. We determine the exact value for all sufficiently large $n$, showing that \[ \ex(n,C_6,C_8)=6\binom{n-3}{3}+12(n-5). \] Moreover, the unique extremal graph is $K_3\vee (K_2\cup I_{n-5})$. The main new ingredient is a codegree decomposition for $C_8$-free graphs: a packing lemma for triangles in the linear-codegree graph recovers an almost spanning common neighborhood, and a defect-absorption argument upgrades this stability to the exact extremal graph.

### [2] [Counting perfect edge dominating sets: extremal results and linear-time algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03894)
*Luciano N. Grippo, Min Chih Lin, Verónica Moyano, Camilo Vera*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 刻画树、森林、chordal 图中 perfect edge dominating set 数量的极值图，并给出 generalized series-parallel 与 chordal 图上的线性时间计数算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: perfect edge dominating set（外部每边被恰一集内边支配）的计数与极值是图论枚举的自然问题。

Method: 极值刻画与动态规划计数。

Result: 刻画树/森林/chordal 图中 perfect edge dominating set 数量的极值图；给出 generalized series-parallel 与 chordal 图上计数 perfect edge dominating set 与 dominating induced matching 的线性时间算法。

Conclusion: perfect edge dominating set 的极值与高效计数在这些图类上被解决。

Future Work: 推广到更广图类；研究平均计数行为。

Abstract: An edge of a graph \emph{dominates} itself and each edge adjacent to it. A \emph{perfect edge dominating set} is a subset of edges such that each edge outside the subset is dominated by exactly one edge of the subset. In this article, we characterize the extremal graphs on $n$ vertices in the classes of trees, forests, and chordal graphs with respect to the number of perfect edge dominating sets. Moreover, we derive linear-time algorithms for counting perfect edge dominating sets and for counting dominating induced matchings in generalized series-parallel graphs and chordal graphs.

### [3] [Eternal domination in Cayley graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04024)
*MacKenzie Carr, Nancy E. Clarke, Gary MacGillivray, Joy Morris*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 研究 Cayley graph 的 eternal domination number，证明有 efficient dominating set 的 vertex-transitive 图其 eternal domination number 等于 domination number，并构造 $\gamma_{\mathrm{all}}^\infty=\gamma+1$、$\ge\gamma+2$ 的 Cayley graph 族。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: eternal domination 建模无限攻击序列下的守卫防御，其在对称 Cayley graph 上的值是自然问题。

Method: 用 efficient dominating set 与 vertex-transitivity；对 generalized dihedral 群 Cayley graph 分析 reflection 数量。

Result: 有 efficient dominating set 的 vertex-transitive 图 $\gamma_{\mathrm{all}}^\infty=\gamma$；generalized dihedral 群且 reflection 少或多的 Cayley graph 可 efficiently dominated；给出 $\gamma_{\mathrm{all}}^\infty=\gamma+1$（推广 Braga 等）与 $\ge\gamma+2$ 的无穷族。

Conclusion: Cayley graph 的 eternal domination number 与 domination number 的关系被刻画，含 gap 为 1、$\ge2$ 的族。

Future Work: 确定更一般 Cayley graph 的 eternal domination number；刻画 gap 的可能取值。

Abstract: Eternal domination is a process in which a set of guards occupying a dominating set on a graph protects against an infinite sequence of attacks. After a vertex is attacked, one guard must move along an edge to the attacked vertex and each of the remaining guards may move along an edge so that the guards again occupy a dominating set on the graph and can defend the next attack. The minimum number of guards needed in a graph $Γ$ is the eternal domination number, denoted by $γ_{\mathrm{all}}^\infty(Γ)$. In this paper, we show that the eternal domination number of a vertex-transitive graph with an efficient dominating set is equal to its domination number. We show that a Cayley graph on a generalized dihedral group whose connection set contains few or many reflections is efficiently dominated. Then, we provide an infinite family of connected Cayley graphs for which $γ_{\mathrm{all}}^{\infty}(Γ) = γ(Γ)+1$, generalizing a result of [Braga et al., J. Combin. Math. Combin. Comput. 96 (2016), 13--22]. Finally, we build an infinite family of connected Cayley graphs with $γ_{\mathrm{all}}^\infty(Γ) \geq γ(Γ)+2$.

</details>

### [4] [Higman--Thompson groups $F_n$ all the way down](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04038)
*Gili Golan*

Main category: math.GR

TL;DR: 证明每个 Higman–Thompson 群 $F_n$（$n\ge2$）都含无限指数、同构于自身的极大子群，并给出一条各同构于 $F_n$、交为平凡的极大子群链。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Thompson 型群的极大子群结构与自相似性是几何群论的核心问题。

Method: 用 semi-synchronizing transducer 与子群的 Stallings 2-core 建立桥梁，把此类 homeomorphism 的共轭归约为算法过程。

Result: $F_n$ 有无限指数、同构于自身的极大子群及链 $F_n=H_0>H_1>\cdots$；刻画把 $F_n$ 共轭进 $F_m$ 的 Cantor 空间同胚为 minimal transducer semi-synchronizing 的有理同胚；证明 Jones 三元 oriented 子群 $\vec F_3\cong F_4$，并证 $F$ 中已知在 $(0,1)$ 上极小作用的无限指数极大子群皆为 Higman–Thompson 群。

Conclusion: Higman–Thompson 群自身可无限层嵌套为极大子群，transducer 方法给出算法性刻画。

Future Work: 是否 $F$ 中所有极小作用的无限指数极大子群都同构于 Higman–Thompson 群；扩展 fast group 的结果。

Abstract: We prove that for every $n\ge 2$ the Higman--Thompson group $F_n$ has a maximal subgroup of infinite index isomorphic to itself. In fact, we construct a chain of subgroups $F_n=H_0>H_1>H_2>\cdots$, all isomorphic to $F_n$ and with trivial intersection, such that for every $i$ the only subgroups of $F_n$ containing $H_i$ are $H_i,H_{i-1},\ldots,H_0=F_n$; in particular, each $H_{i+1}$ is maximal in $H_i$. We prove that for all $n\ge m\ge 2$, every closed maximal subgroup of $F_m$ isomorphic to $F_n$ arises from a homeomorphism between the $n$-ary and $m$-ary Cantor spaces given by a finite semi-synchronizing transducer--a variation of the synchronizing transducers of Bleak, Cameron, Maissel, Navas and Olukoya. We characterize the homeomorphisms of Cantor spaces conjugating $F_n$ into $F_m$ as the order-preserving or order-reversing rational homeomorphisms whose minimal transducer is semi-synchronizing. At the heart of the paper is a machinery bridging transducers and Stallings $2$-cores of subgroups, which reduces the conjugation of finitely generated closed subgroups by such homeomorphisms to an algorithmic procedure. As applications, we prove that Jones' ternary oriented subgroup $\vec F_3\le F_3$ is isomorphic to $F_4$, answering questions of Aiello, and that all known maximal subgroups of infinite index of Thompson's group $F$ which act minimally on $(0,1)$ are isomorphic to Higman--Thompson groups. That raises the problem of whether all maximal subgroups of infinite index of $F$ which act minimally on $(0,1)$ are isomorphic to Higman--Thompson groups. We briefly discuss related results regarding fast groups of homeomorphisms and maximal subgroups of Thompson groups.

</details>
