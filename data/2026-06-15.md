### [1] [Ramsey-Turán Type Problem for Perfect Transitive Triangle Tilings in Digraphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14161)
*Zhimin Wang, Zhilan Wang, Jin Yan*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文在有向图中研究完美传递三角形铺装的 Ramsey-Tur\'an 型问题，证明若 $n$ 顶点有向图 $D$ 满足 $\alpha(D)\leq o(1)n$ 和 $\delta(D)\geq (1+o(1))n$，则 $D$ 具有完美 $T_3$-铺装，该最小度条件渐近最优。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 经典的 Corr\'adi-Hajnal 定理给出图中完美三角形铺装的最小度条件。Balogh、Molla 和 Sharifzadeh 加入独立数条件降低了度下界。本文将其推广到有向图中传递三角形的完美铺装，填补了有向图铺装理论中 Ramsey-Tur\'an 型结果的空白。

Method: 使用极值图论和 Ramsey-Tur\'an 理论的方法，结合有向图的结构分析和吸收方法（absorption method），在独立数条件和最小度条件的共同约束下构造完美 $T_3$-铺装。

Result: 证明若 $D$ 是 $n$ 顶点有向图，满足 $\alpha(D)\leq o(1)n$ 和 $\delta(D)\geq (1+o(1))n$，则 $D$ 具有完美 $T_3$-铺装（$T_3$ 为传递三角形）。该最小度条件渐近最优。该结果蕴含 Balogh、Molla 和 Sharifzadeh 关于无向图完美三角形铺装的定理。

Conclusion: 该结果成功将 Ramsey-Tur\'an 型铺装问题从无向图推广到有向图，且最小度条件在渐近意义下不可改进。这为进一步研究有向图中一般 $H$-因子的 Ramsey-Tur\'an 条件奠定了基础。

Future Work: 能否取消渐近条件得到精确的最小度阈值？对于一般的 $k$-顶点传递竞赛图 $T_k$ 的完美铺装，相应的 Ramsey-Tur\'an 条件是什么？其他有向子图的铺装问题值得探索。

Abstract: The classical Corrádi-Hajnal theorem states that for any multiple $n$ of $3$, if $G$ is a graph with $n$ vertices and $\delta(G) \geq 2n/3$, then $G$ can be partitioned into $n/3$ vertex-disjoint copies of the triangle [\emph{Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hung.}, 14:423-439, 1964]. Balogh, Molla and Sharifzadeh obtained a smaller lower bound by adding the independence number condition [\emph{Random Struct. Algorithms}, 49:669-693, 2016]. In this paper, we study perfect tilings in digraphs subject to conditions on the independence number and the degree. The independence number, $\alpha(D)$, of $D$ is the maximum integer $k$ such that $D$ has an independent set of cardinality $k$. We show that if $D$ is an $n$-vertex digraph with $\alpha(D)\leq o(1)n$ and $\delta(D) \geq (1+o(1))n$, then $D$ has a perfect $T_3$-tiling, where $T_3$ denotes a transitive triangle. This minimum degree condition is asymptotically best possible. Moreover, our result implies the theorem of Balogh, Molla, and Sharifzadeh concerning perfect triangle tilings.

### [2] [The maximum number of edges in $C_{2k+1}^{-}$-free unbalanced signed graphs with bounded clique number](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14381)
*Junjie Wang, Yaoping Hou*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究具有有界团数的非平衡$C_{2k+1}^{-}$-free符号图的Turán型问题，利用经典稳定性定理确定了最大边数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 有界团数图的Turán问题最近受到广泛关注。由于普通图可视为无负边的符号图，将有界团数的Turán问题推广到符号图是一个自然的方向，尤其是对于带限制子结构的非平衡符号图。

Method: 利用经典稳定性定理（stability theorem），将问题转化为对底层图不含color-critical图$F$的结构分析；结合极值图论中针对非平衡符号图的计数和构造技术。

Result: 对充分大的$n$和color-critical图$F$，确定了所有$n$个顶点、$C_{2k+1}^{-}$-free且底层图$F$-free的非平衡符号图的最大边数。

Conclusion: 该结果将有界团数的Turán问题成功推广至符号图领域，为符号图的极值理论提供了新的框架和工具。

Future Work: 将结果推广到其他禁用的符号子图$H$，以及考虑平衡符号图的情形；进一步探讨更一般的图类（如超图或有向图）中类似极值问题的推广。

Abstract: Recently, the Turán problem for graphs with bounded clique number has attracted considerable attention. Since a graph can be regarded as a signed graph without negative edges, it is natural to extend the study of such Turán-type problems to signed graphs. With this motivation, we investigate the Turán-type problem for unbalanced $C_{2k+1}^{-}$-free signed graphs with bounded clique number. In fact, we establish a general result via the classical stability theorem. Specifically, for sufficiently large $n$ and a color-critical graph $F$, we determine the maximum number of edges among all $n$-vertex $C_{2k+1}^{-}$-free unbalanced signed graphs whose underlying graphs are $F$-free.

### [3] [Commutative Distance-Regularity: Algebraic Hierarchies and Cayley Graph Constructions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14538)
*Agustín Oromí, Adrián Pastine*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文解决了关于图的距离正则性在图乘积下代数与度量继承的若干公开问题，引入并研究了距离游走正则图这一新的图族，并通过层层提升操作构造了无穷多族图。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 距离正则图及其变体（CDDR、DMR、DDR）在代数图论中有重要地位，但它们在图乘积（尤其是直积）下的闭包性不明确。填补这些拓扑间隙并构建系统的正则性层次结构是该文的动机。

Method: 刻画CDDR、DMR和DDR图在强积和直积下的行为；为处理直积下非闭包问题，引入距离游走正则（DWR）图族；定义$i$-CDDR图并放松全局交换性条件；通过Cayley图上的系统提升操作（lifting operations）构造无穷图族。

Result: 完整刻画了CDDR、DMR、DDR图在强积和直积下的保持性；引入DWR图族解决了直积下正则性闭包问题；在Cayley图框架下结构化了$i$-CDDR图类并构造了任意大阶数和直径的无穷图族。

Conclusion: 该工作系统化了距离正则性的代数层次，引入的新图族填补了现有理论中的拓扑空隙，为代数图论中的正则性研究提供了统一框架。

Future Work: 进一步研究DWR图在其他图乘积（如字典积、笛卡尔积）下的行为；探索$i$-CDDR图在非Cayley图上的推广；研究这些正则性类与图谱理论的关系。

Abstract: This paper resolves several open questions regarding the algebraic and metric inheritance of distance-based regularities under graph products, with a particular focus on Commutative Distance Degree-Regular (CDDR) graphs. We characterize the behavior of CDDR, distance mean-regular (DMR), and distance degree-regular (DDR) graphs under the strong and direct (tensor) products. To address the non-closure of classical regularities under the direct product, we introduce and study a novel, broader family of graphs: \textit{Distance Walk Regular (DWR)} graphs. Furthermore, we relax the global commutativity condition by defining and structuralizing the class of $i$-CDDR graphs, providing deep characterizations within the realm of Cayley graphs. By developing systematic lifting operations, we construct infinite families of graphs with arbitrarily large order and diameter within specific structural intersections, effectively settling topological gaps in the current regularity hierarchy.

### [4] [Upper tails for irregular graphs beyond the mean-field regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14564)
*Asaf Cohen Antonir, Matan Harel, Frank Mousset, Wojciech Samotij*

Main category: math.PR

TL;DR: 本文在超越平均场区域（beyond mean-field regime）的边密度范围内，证明了连通非正则图 $H$ 在 $G_{n,p}$ 中计数的上尾概率的渐近紧界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 随机图 $G_{n,p}$ 中子图计数的上尾概率是极值组合与概率论的核心问题；正则图的 mean-field 区域已有较完整结果（通过 Chatterjee-Varadhan 变分公式），而非正则图在稀疏区域的 upper tail 行为之前缺乏精确刻画。

Method: 引入一个新的变分问题，在 naive mean-field 近似的组合优化项基础上加入熵项（entropy term），该熵项对应于 $K_n$ 中某些高度结构化图的大量嵌入；对「稳定图」（stable graphs）证明该变分描述在最优密度范围内有效；对更特殊的子类得到最优常数界。

Result: 对最大度 $\Delta \geq 2$ 的连通非正则图 $H$，当 $p \geq n^{-1/\Delta - \varepsilon_H} (\log n)^{\omega(1)}$ 时给出了 $\log\Pr(X_H \geq (1+\delta)\mathbb{E}[X_H])$ 的渐近界；对稳定图类，该描述在至多差 poly($\log\log n$) 因子的密度范围内有效；对所有非正则完全二部图和更广泛的稳定图子类，密度范围至多差一个常数因子即为最优。

Conclusion: 将子图计数 upper tail 的变分刻画从正则图推广到非正则图，揭示了非正则性引入的额外熵效应，大幅推进了该方向的理解；对于不规则图的完整刻画仍有 gap。

Future Work: 消除稳定图中 poly($\log\log n$) 的因子达到真正最优；推广到不连通非正则图的情形；研究能否用类似方法处理其他随机图模型（如均匀随机图 $G_{n,m}$）的 upper tail 问题。

Abstract: Let $G_{n,p}$ be the binomial random graph of density $p$ and let $X_H$ be the number of copies of a fixed graph $H$ in $G_{n,p}$. We prove asymptotically tight bounds on the logarithmic upper-tail probability of $X_H$ whenever $H$ is a connected, irregular graph with maximum degree $\Delta \ge 2$ and $p \ge n^{-1/\Delta - \varepsilon_H} (\log n)^{\omega(1)}$ for an explicit $\varepsilon_H >0$. These bounds are expressed in terms of a new variational problem that generalises the combinatorial optimisation problem arising from the naïve mean-field approximation. This new variational problem includes an entropy term that corresponds to the large number of embeddings of certain highly structured graphs in $K_n$. For a certain class of irregular graphs $H$ that we call stable, we show that this description of the upper-tail probability is valid in a range of densities that is optimal up to a poly($\log\log n$) factor. For a further subclass of stable graphs, which includes all irregular complete bipartite graphs, we show that this range of densities is optimal up to a multiplicative constant.

</details>

### [5] [Three subgroup common transversal in abelian groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14319)
*Maria Loukaki, Emmanouil Spyridakis*

Main category: math.GR

TL;DR: 本文完全刻画了有限 Abel 群中三个等阶子群何时共享公共 transversal，并由此解决了 $n=3$ 情形下一个 1997 年提出的开问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 有限 Abel 群中子群的公共 transversal 问题等价于 $\mathbb{R}^d$ 中等体积满秩格共享公共基本域的问题；$n=2$ 的情形早已解决，$n=3$ 的情形自 1997 年以来一直是开问题。

Method: 利用有限 Abel 群的分解理论，将三个等阶子群共享 transversal 的条件转化为一系列初等数论和线性代数条件，进行完全的分类讨论。

Result: 给出了有限 Abel 群中三个等阶子群有公共 transversal 的充要条件的完整刻画；由此确定了 $\mathbb{R}^d$ 中三个等体积、离散和的满秩格何时有公共基本域，解决了 $n=3$ 情形的开问题。

Conclusion: 为 1997 年开问题给出了 $n=3$ 的完整解答，但 $n \geq 4$ 时该问题仍然开放，且复杂度可能大幅增加。

Future Work: 将刻画推广到 $n \geq 4$ 个子群/格的情形；探索非 Abel 群中子群的公共 transversal 问题。

Abstract: We completely characterize when three equal-order subgroups of a finite abelian group share a common transversal. Consequently, we determine when three full-rank lattices of equal volume with a discrete sum in $\mathbb{R}^d$ admit a common fundamental domain, answering, for $n=3$, an open question from 1997.

</details>
