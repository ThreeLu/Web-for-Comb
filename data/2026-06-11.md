### [1] [On Balance, To What Degree is Burr's Conjecture True?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11410)
*Shagnik Das, Bruce Reed, Jozef Skokan*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文基本解决了Burr猜想对不均衡树的情形：当t2≥2t1时存在反例并给出偏离量级；当t2远大于t1时确定了使Burr界紧的最大度阈值约为t2-t1。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Burr猜想（1976年）是树Ramsey数的核心问题。虽然已知某些(t1,t2)对存在反例，但对不均衡树的完整刻画一直悬而未决。本文旨在确定Burr界紧或不紧的条件，厘清分歧比和最大度的作用。

Method: 通过构造反例族和Ramsey下界证明：利用组合设计、概率方法和极值图论工具给出存在反例的充分条件，并量化Burr界与实际Ramsey数之间的偏离量级；另一方面证明在一定最大度条件下Burr界是紧的。

Result: (a) t2≥2t1时始终存在反例，偏离量级为max(t1²/t2,√t1)；(b) t2≥500t1时，若Δ(T)≤t2-t1则Burr界紧，若Δ(T)远大于t2-t1则偏离至少C log t2。特别地，当Δ(T)≈t/3时Burr界可不成立。

Conclusion: 不均衡树的Ramsey数行为由最大度与t2-t1的关系决定。Burr猜想在t2远大于t1且最大度不太大的情形下（渐近）成立，但在多数其他情形下有本质偏离。

Future Work: 压缩t2/t1和最大度阈值之间的间隙（例如从t2≥500t1降低到更实际的比例）；研究中等规模最大度情形下的紧界或更精确的渐近公式。

Abstract: For many trees $T$, the Ramsey number of $T$, denoted by ${\mathcal R}(T)$, is determined by the sizes of the partition classes in its unique bipartition. In 1976, Burr proved that when $T$ has partition classes of size $t_1$ and $t_2$ with $t_1 \le t_2$, the Ramsey number is at least $\max(2t_2-1,2t_1+t_2-1)$, and conjectured that this is tight. While counterexamples have been found for some pairs $(t_1, t_2)$, a main focus of research on this problem has been determining ratios $t_2/t_1$ or bounds on the maximum degree of $T$ for which Burr's bound is either exactly or asymptotically tight. We essentially resolve these questions for lopsided trees. Specifically, we show that (a) there are counterexamples whenever $t_2 \ge 2t_1$, with the order of magnitude of the difference between the largest Ramsey numbers and Burr's bound being $\max \left( t_1^2/t_2, \sqrt{t_1} \right)$, and (b) for $t_2 \ge 500 t_1$, Burr's bound is tight when $\Delta(T) \le t_2 - t_1$, but is off by at least $C \log t_2$ (even when $t_2 \ge 2 t_1$) when $\Delta(T) \gtrsim t_2 - t_1$. In particular, this shows that Burr's bound need not hold for $t$-vertex trees $T$ with $\Delta(T) \approx t/3$.
