### [1] [Universal Vertices and Saturation Numbers for Disjoint Triangles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25321)
*Xiaoteng Zhou, Naoyuki Kamiyama*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 确定了 $(m+1)K_3$-saturated 图的 saturation 数：对 $n\ge9m+5$ 有 $\operatorname{sat}((m+1)K_3,n)=n+6m-1$，并把 Faudree 等人的阈值从 $n\ge12m+3$ 降到 $n\ge9m+5$。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: 不交三角形并 $(m+1)K_3$ 的 saturation 数是极值饱和理论的自然对象，Faudree 等人只对充分大 $n$ 给出结果。

Method: 构造一族 $(m+1)K_3$-saturated 图给出 $O(n^{3/2})$ 的一致上界；分析删去所有满度顶点后分量的结构限制。

Result: $\operatorname{sat}((m+1)K_3,n)=n+6m-1$（$n\ge9m+5$）；对所有 $m\ge1$、$n\ge3m+3$ 有一致上界 $O(n^{3/2})$；给出两条分量结构限制。

Conclusion: 不交三角形的 saturation 数有精确线性公式，且适用阈值被显著降低。

Future Work: 进一步降低阈值至最优；推广到 $(m+1)K_p$（$p\ge4$）的精确 saturation 数。

Abstract: A graph $G$ is $\mathcal F$-saturated if $G$ contains no member of $\mathcal F$, but the addition of any non-edge creates a copy of a member of $\mathcal F$. For $m\ge 1$, let $(m+1)K_3$ denote the vertex-disjoint union of $(m+1)$ triangles. In this paper, we study $(m+1)K_3$-saturated graphs. We construct a family of $(m+1)K_3$-saturated graphs which gives the uniform upper bound $\operatorname{sat}((m+1)K_3,n)=O(n^{3/2})$ for all $m\ge 1$ and $n\ge 3m+3$. For general $(m+1)K_p$-saturated graphs, Faudree et al. determined $\operatorname{sat}((m+1)K_p, n)$ for sufficiently large $n$. In the case of $p=3$, we lower Faudree's threshold from $n \ge 12m + 3$ to $n\ge 9m+5$ and prove that $\operatorname{sat}((m+1)K_3,n)=n+6m-1.$ We also provide two structural restrictions on the components obtained after deleting all vertices of degree $|V(G)|-1$ from an $(m+1)K_3$-saturated graph.

### [2] [Winding number and circular coloring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25644)
*Reza Naserasr, Cyril Pujol, Lujia Wang*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 用 winding number 框架统一并简化了 circular chromatic number 的 gap 现象，推广了 Youngs 关于射影平面 quadrangulation 着色数的定理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Youngs 定理表明射影平面 quadrangulation 的着色数不为 3，其 circular 版本给出 $(2,4)$ 的 gap；此前缺乏统一框架。

Method: 把着色延拓为连续映射，用 winding number 处理带一组指定有向偶圈的图；对全偶面与全奇面情形分别建立 gap。

Result: 给出 circular chromatic number gap 的统一简化证明；例如射影平面上全为 5-圈面的图，其 circular chromatic number 要么为 $5/2$（当且仅当 Eulerian 且每条不可缩面走为奇长）要么至少 3。

Conclusion: winding number 为曲面嵌入图的 circular 着色 gap 提供了统一视角，并把结果从偶面推广到奇面。

Future Work: 推广到更一般的面长组合；探索高亏格曲面上的 gap 结构。

Abstract: In 1996, Youngs proved a surprising theorem that quadrangulations of the projective plane could never have chromatic number exactly 3. This sparked a lot of interest, and the result has been further developed in many directions over the past decades. For example, the result is strengthened by considering the circular chromatic number, which is a real-valued lower bound on the chromatic number. The circular chromatic number of a quadrangulation cannot be in the interval (2,4). This parameter allows a generalization to larger even faces, for which a similar gap exists. In this work, we place these results into a framework based on the notion of winding number using extensions of colorings to continuous mappings. This yields unified and simplified proofs of gaps in the circular chromatic number for graphs with a distinguished set of directed even cycles. This generalizes the setting of graphs embedded on surfaces where every face is even. We further establish an analogous gap phenomenon when all faces are of a given odd length, previously known only in the case of triangulations. For example, we conclude that if G is a graph embedded on the projective plane such that all faces are 5-cycles, then either its circular chromatic number is 5/2 or at least 3, the former being the case only if G is Eulerian and every noncontractible facial walk is of odd length...

### [3] [A lifting theorem for generalized Turán numbers of triangles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25785)
*Junjie Wang, Yaoping Hou*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 给出三角形 generalized Turán 数的 lifting 原理：在局部邻域强制条件下，单个禁止图 $F$ 的渐近值可提升到不交并 $(s+1)F$ 的 $\operatorname{ex}(n,K_3,(s+1)F)$。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: generalized Turán 数 $\operatorname{ex}(n,H,F)$ 对不交并型禁止图缺乏统一处理手段。

Method: 关键假设是存在 $\operatorname{ex}(n,R)=o(n^2)$ 的 $R$ 使 $F\subseteq K_1\nabla R$；据此把单禁止图渐近与同时达到极值三角形/边密度的构造提升到不交并情形，并给出加权表达式的精确版本。

Result: 在邻域强制条件下得到 $\operatorname{ex}(n,K_3,(s+1)F)$ 的渐近（及精确）值；应用于路与星的 suspension 的不交并，并复原不交奇圈的已知精确结果。

Conclusion: lifting 原理把不交并禁止图的三角形计数归约为单个禁止图，统一处理了多个极值问题。

Future Work: 推广到 $H\ne K_3$ 的 generalized Turán 计数；放宽邻域强制条件。

Abstract: For graphs $H$ and $F$, the generalized Turán number $\operatorname{ex}(n,H,F)$ denotes the maximum number of copies of $H$ in an $n$-vertex $F$-free graph. We prove a general lifting principle for the case $H=K_3$ and the forbidden graph is a vertex-disjoint union of several copies of a graph. The key hypothesis is a local neighborhood-forcing condition: there is a graph $R$ with $\operatorname{ex}(n,R)=o(n^2)$ such that $F\subseteq K_1\nabla R$. Under this condition, the corresponding single-forbidden-graph asymptotics, together with a construction attaining the relevant extremal triangle and edge densities simultaneously, lift to an asymptotic value for \(\operatorname{ex}(n,K_3,(s+1)F)\) for every integer \(s \). We also prove an exact version in terms of the maximum value of a weighted expression over all graphs of a given size that avoid the forbidden graph. As applications, we obtain exact or asymptotic results for disjoint unions of suspensions of paths and stars. We also recover known exact results for disjoint odd cycles.

### [5] [A Simple Counting Argument for Dense Linear Hypergraphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25931)
*Lior Gishboliner, József Solymosi*

Main category: math.CO

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: Brown–Erdős–Sós 猜想及其密度形式是超图极值核心，已有证明较复杂。

Result: 若线性 $r$-uniform 超图边数超过给定阈值，则含 $k$ 条边张成至多 $(r-2)k+3$ 个顶点；给出密度阈值 $c\ge\frac{r-1}{r}\cdot\frac{k-2}{(r-2)(k-2)+1}+o(1)$；triple system 情形改进 Santos–Tyomkyn 的 $4/5$ 为 $\frac{2(k-2)}{3(k-1)}+o(1)$，并简证 Keevash–Long 的大 uniformity 结果。

Conclusion: 一个简单的局部平均论证即可给出并改进多个 Brown–Erdős–Sós 型密度界。

In particular, this yields a simple proof of the large-uniformity form of the Brown-Erdős-Sós theorem, due to Keevash and Long. In the case of triple systems, our bound becomes $c \geq \frac{2(k-2)}{3(k-1)} + o(1)$, improving upon a bound of $\frac{4}{5}$ due to Santos and Tyomkyn.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
