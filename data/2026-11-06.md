### [1] [Hypergraphs with zero uniform Turán density at intermediate levels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06964)
*Heng Li, Xizhi Liu*

Main category: math.CO

TL;DR: 本文刻画了在中间层级 $1\le j\le r-3$ 上，$r$-一致超图的 uniform Turán density 为零的充要条件，即 $F$ 存在一个顶点排序使得任意两条边共享的每个 $(j+1)$-集在两条边中占据相同位置。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Motivation: uniform Turán density 是超图 Turán 问题在均匀分布意义下的推广。Erdős 在 $j=0$ 以及 Reiher、Rödl 和 Schacht 在 $j=r-2$ 时分别给出了零密度的刻画，而中间层级 $1\le j\le r-3$ 此前完全未知；本文填补了这一空白，并验证了 Reiher–Rödl–Schacht 提出的推广猜想。

Method: 证明结合了 hypergraph regularity（超图正则引理）与 spreadable random hypergraphs 中的 adjacent exchanges 技术，用以处理中间层级上 $(j+1)$-集位置一致性的组合约束。

Result: 对任意 $r\ge4$ 与 $1\le j\le r-3$，$F$ 的 level-$j$ uniform Turán density 为零当且仅当 $F$ 存在顶点排序，使得任意两条边共享的每个 $(j+1)$-集在两条边中占据相同位置。由此推出：在 level $j$ 上，任意（可能无限的）禁用族的正 uniform Turán density 至少为 $N^{-N}$，其中 $N=\binom r{j+1}$。

Conclusion: 该结果统一并推广了 $j=0$ 与 $j=r-2$ 的经典零密度刻画，完整解决了中间层级的零密度判定问题，并给出了正密度的一般下界 $N^{-N}$，深化了对 uniform Turán density 离散结构的理解。

Future Work: 论文未明确列出 open problems；自然的后续方向包括确定正 uniform Turán density 的更精确下界或具体值，以及将零密度刻画推广到非一致或更一般的禁用族设定。

Abstract: An $r$-uniform hypergraph $H$ is uniformly dense with respect to $j$-graphs if, up to a small error, at least a prescribed proportion of the $r$-cliques of every $j$-uniform hypergraph on the same vertex set are edges of $H$. For a forbidden hypergraph $F$, its uniform Turán density at level $j$ is the supremum of these density bounds over arbitrarily large $F$-free hypergraphs with the error tending to zero. For every $r\ge4$ and $1\le j\le r-3$, this paper proves that this Turán density is zero precisely when $F$ admits a vertex ordering in which every $(j+1)$-set shared by two edges occupies the same positions in both edges. This generalizes the zero-density characterizations of Erdős at $j=0$ and of Reiher, Rödl, and Schacht at $j=r-2$, and establishes the latter authors' proposed extension to the remaining nontrivial levels. Consequently, at level $j$, every positive uniform Turán density of a possibly infinite forbidden family is at least $N^{-N}$, where $N=\binom r{j+1}$. The proof combines hypergraph regularity with adjacent exchanges in spreadable random hypergraphs.

  <summary>Details</summary>
